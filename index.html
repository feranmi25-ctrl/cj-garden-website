Frequently asked questions

How much does a clearance cost?

Prices vary with size and complexity. We provide free local quotes — send a photo and postcode or book a quick site visit for a firm price.

How quickly can you start?

We often have short-notice availability. When you get in touch we’ll tell you the earliest convenient date.

Do you remove the green waste?

Yes — green waste is included in most jobs. We’ll confirm whether an extra skip is needed during the quote if necessary.

Do you handle very overgrown gardens?

Yes — rescuing overgrown gardens is a core service. We’ll assess access, waste volume and hazards when we quote.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept card and bank transfer. Cash on completion is also available by arrangement. (Showroom example.)
